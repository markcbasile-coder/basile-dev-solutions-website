Hi, I’m Mark Basile, the developer and founder behind Basile Dev Solutions.

My path into tech started early, learning the basics of JavaScript during my IT program at the Atlantic County Institute of Technology, and later graduating from ECPI University in 2018 with my B.S. in Electrical Engineering (Mechatronics Concentration). From there, I spent years working the IT helpdesk trenches, troubleshooting everyday tech issues, with a deeper dive into platform administration and integrations toward the end of my time at Agile Defense. Alongside my support background, I've put in the self-directed study to expand my technical toolkit, earning 20 credentials in the ServiceNow ecosystem (including my Certified Application Developer certification) and completing my Responsive Web Design certification through freeCodeCamp.

I started Basile Dev Solutions to combine that practical troubleshooting mindset with clean, modern web development. Whether you need a custom site or a hands-off Website-as-a-Service (WaaS) plan, my goal is to deliver reliable tech solutions without the runaround, so you can focus entirely on running your business.
